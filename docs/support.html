Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Find BreathFlow and tap Cancel Subscription.

The app is not syncing with Apple Health

Make sure Health access is enabled: go to iPhone Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > BreathFlow, and enable Mindful Minutes.

How do I change the language?

BreathFlow follows your device language. To change it, go to iPhone Settings > General > Language & Region.
